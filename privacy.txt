1. What the app is
OpenWebSDR is a client for OpenWebRX SDR receivers. It connects to servers you add, lets you listen and (if you choose) record audio, manage favorites, and scan frequencies.

2. Data we process
All data stays on your device, except what is sent to servers you choose.

Stored on the phone (local storage):

server list (address, username, password, optional admin key)
favorites, receiver state, settings
audio files if you press REC
scanner hit history
We do not create an account on our servers. We do not sell data. We do not use ads or third-party trackers.

3. Network connections
The app only connects to OpenWebRX / HTTP servers you configure (audio, spectrum, chat, radio commands) and, if you enter credentials, to that server’s admin panel.

Whoever runs that server sees what OpenWebRX provides. Their privacy policy is theirs, not ours.

4. Audio
Recording starts only if you press REC or enable VOX. Files stay on the device. We do not upload them to the cloud.

5. Location
The app does not use the phone GPS. Any map/positions (e.g. APRS) come from radio data decoded by the server, not from your location.

6. Android permissions

Network: connect to servers
Notifications: scanner alert, if enabled
Storage: recordings and favorites
Audio: radio stream only if you use REC, not the environment microphone
Check permissions in Android Settings and on the Play listing.

7. Retention and deletion
Data stays as long as you keep the app. Uninstalling deletes it (except files you copied elsewhere). You can delete favorites and servers in the app.

8. Children
The app is not intended for children under 13.

Updates to this policy will be posted at this same URL with a new date.
